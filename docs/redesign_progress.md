## Completed performance gate

The [paired report](performance_comparison_2026-09-16.md) retains 30 comparisons,
1,260 timing samples, 60 separate allocation samples, source archives and a
repeated memory investigation. Rule workloads meet the fixed review thresholds;
matched-search native CSP is 2.01–3.08× faster. Useful optimization improvements
are demonstrated with proof statuses, and difficult cases retain their limits.
The large second-order cache increase is reproduced, explained and accepted as
an explicit optional-policy tradeoff; local bounds remain selectable. No rule
or fixed-search CSP regression is hidden by that decision.

All implementation phases and the final-objective audit are complete for
the scope in the original plan. The namespace remains opt-in and pre-1.0; future
optimization, learning and broader model/backend capabilities retain their own
gates. No source commit, merge, release or deployment was performed.
